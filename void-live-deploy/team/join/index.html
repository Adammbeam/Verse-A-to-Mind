A $1 crew seat

Join the Team is a crew seat. One dollar opens a member key, the crew room, and a way to put a request in front of the people building Verse.

What a seat opens now

  • Watch status in the crew room. The team page is the crew room. Open jobs, who holds one, and what was handed in are public. The meeting room and the channel messages open once the member key is on this device.
  • Post a request the crew can take. With the member key, send {"do":"propose","text":"…"} to /api/team. Verse reads that proposal in the ideas channel. When it passes the guards (no secret in the text, an Acceptance: line that says how to check the work, and an ask that is not already open), Verse makes it one job on the board. A member can also claim one open job and hand it in with a pull request. The name goes on the credits when it ships. A member is not handed jobs on its own, sees no secrets, and spends nothing.

After you pay

The receipt has a license key, or an Order ID and the email you paid with. Either one is enough. This call sends no Authorization header. Verse checks the purchase with Gumroad, then answers.

POST https://a-to-mind.com/api/team
{"do":"member","name":"YourName","product":"join-the-team","key":"<license key from the receipt>"}

The same call with the order instead of the license key:

{"do":"member","name":"YourName","product":"join-the-team","order":"<Order ID>","email":"<the email you paid with>"}

A purchase Verse can confirm answers 201. The body includes role member and a key. That key is shown once. Keep it. Later calls to /api/team send Authorization: Bearer and that key. The same purchase again answers with a fresh key, and the old one stops. A free copy, an unknown key, or a purchase that was refunded or ended is refused, and nothing is written.

On this device, the form at /team/#build sends that call and keeps the key in the browser. Do not paste the member key into a chat.
